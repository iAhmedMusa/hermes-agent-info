Privacy Policy

Hermes Agent · Last updated: 9 October 2026

Summary: Hermes Agent is a single-user personal tool. It accesses only the Google account of the person who installs and authorises it. It does not collect data from anyone else, does not transmit data to any third party for advertising or profiling, and does not sell data to anyone.

1. Who this applies to

Hermes Agent ("the application") is a self-hosted personal assistant operated by Ahmed Musa for his own use. There are no other users. This policy describes how the application handles data belonging to the single account that authorises it.

2. What data is accessed

When the owner authorises the application, it may access the following from the owner's own Google account:

Google serviceWhat it is used for
GmailReading the owner's mail to find and summarise correspondence, and sending messages the owner has personally reviewed and approved.
Google DriveUploading and retrieving the owner's own documents, such as CVs.
Google DocsReading the owner's source documents to reuse their content and formatting.
Google SheetsReading and updating a personal tracking spreadsheet owned by the owner.
CalendarReading schedule entries to produce reminders.
Contacts (read-only)Reading contact details to address correspondence correctly.

3. Where data is stored

The application runs on hardware owned and physically controlled by the owner. Any data it retrieves is held on that machine, and in the owner's own Google account and the owner's own self-hosted file storage. No copy is sent to any external server operated by anyone else.

4. Credentials

The OAuth token issued by Google is stored in a file on the owner's own machine, readable only by the owner's operating system account. It is never transmitted anywhere, never shared, and never written into documents or messages.

5. What is never done

  • Data is never sold, rented, traded or shared with any third party.
  • Data is never used for advertising, profiling or analytics.
  • Data is never used to train machine-learning models.
  • No other person's Google account is ever accessed.
  • No email is sent without the owner's explicit, case-by-case approval of the final text.

6. Third-party processing

The application uses large language model APIs to generate and summarise text. Only the specific content needed for a given task is sent to those providers, and it is used solely to produce the requested output. Credentials, passwords and OAuth tokens are never included.

7. Data retention and deletion

Data retrieved from Google is kept only as long as the owner needs it, and the owner can delete it at any time by removing the local files. The owner may revoke the application's access entirely and immediately at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which stops all further access.

8. Compliance with Google API Services User Data Policy

The application's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new revision date.
